Renovation starts with demolition: old tiles, marble, bricks and walls. If the container is not on site from day one, rubble piles up in front of the house and slows the work. These steps get rubble removal ready before the contractor starts.

## 1. Order the container before demolition starts

Send the work area and the date on WhatsApp +965 97502760 before work begins, and we will confirm availability and the delivery time. We serve every area of Kuwait, daily from 7 am to 10 pm.

## 2. Choose the size by waste type

- [12 m³ rubble container](/en/rubble-container-12m3/): for rubble, bricks, marble and construction and renovation waste.
- [6 m³ skip](/en/skip-6m3/): our smallest container.
- [30 m³ container](/en/container-30m3/): for wood, cardboard and tyres, such as old doors and cupboards and the cartons of new materials.

If you are not sure, send the type of work and we will suggest a size.

## 3. Prepare a place for the container

Pick a spot in front of the site that the delivery truck can reach to set the container down and lift it again, and tell us about anything particular to the site when you order.

In multi-storey buildings the contractor fits a rubble chute that drops the waste straight into the container, as you can see in [our work photos](/en/our-work/).

## 4. Keep rubble separate from wood and cardboard

Heavy rubble goes in the rubble container; wood and cardboard go in the 30 m³ container. See [sorting construction waste](/en/guides/sorting-construction-waste/).

## 5. Ask for a swap when it is full

When the container is full, message us on WhatsApp. We take it away and leave an empty one, so the work carries on without stopping.

## 6. Pickup when the work is finished

When the renovation is done, we collect the container from the site.
