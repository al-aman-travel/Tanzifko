We do not publish a fixed price list, because the rental price changes from one order to another. This page explains what sets the price and what we need from you to send a quote on WhatsApp.

## What sets the rental price?

- **Container size:** we rent a 6 m³ skip, a 12 m³ rubble container and a 30 m³ container, each with its own price. See the [container size guide](/en/container-sizes/) if you are not sure which one you need.
- **Waste type:** rubble, bricks, marble and construction waste go in a 6 or 12 m³ container. Wood, cardboard and tyres go in the 30 m³ container.
- **Work area:** we serve every area of Kuwait across all six governorates, and we need the area name to set the price and the delivery time.
- **Type of contract:** a one-off order, or a monthly or annual contract for companies and projects.

## What does the service cover?

We deliver the container to your site, swap it for an empty one when it is full, and collect it when the work is finished. When you ask for a price, mention how long the work will take and how many swaps you expect, so the offer is clear from the start.

## What to send to get a price

- The work area
- The type of waste
- The size you need, or the type of work so we can suggest a size
- The delivery date you need
- How long the work is expected to take

Send them on WhatsApp +965 97502760 or through the [container request form](/en/request/), and we will reply with the price and the delivery time. We work daily from 7 am to 10 pm.

## Before you order

- Choose the container by waste type first, then by quantity, so you order the right size the first time.
- Keep rubble separate from wood and cardboard: each has its own container. See [sorting construction waste](/en/guides/sorting-construction-waste/).
- For long projects and contracting companies, ask about a monthly or annual contract on the [contractors page](/en/contractors/).
