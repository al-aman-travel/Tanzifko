Choosing a container starts with the type of waste, before the quantity. Heavy waste has one container; light, bulky waste has another. This split helps you order the right container the first time.

## Heavy waste: the 6 or 12 m³ rubble container

- Rubble
- Bricks
- Marble
- Construction and renovation waste

These go in the [12 m³ rubble container](/en/rubble-container-12m3/), or the [6 m³ skip](/en/skip-6m3/) for smaller quantities.

## Light, bulky waste: the 30 m³ container

- Wood
- Cardboard
- Tyres

These go in the [30 m³ container](/en/container-30m3/), which measures 6 × 2.30 × 2.1 m. You can see it at warehouses and industrial areas in [our work photos](/en/our-work/).

## A site with both types

Many renovation and finishing sites produce both: rubble from demolition, then wood and cardboard from finishing and deliveries. In that case order a container for each type, or start with the rubble container during demolition and order the 30 m³ container for the finishing stage.
